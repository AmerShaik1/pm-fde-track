Dashboard Glossary — Terms You'll See at Any Product Org
Funnel
The sequence of steps a user moves through toward a valuable action, where each step loses some % of users. The step with the sharpest drop, not the overall conversion number, is usually where the real problem lives.
Activation
The moment a user first experiences real value — not just signing up. Picking too early a definition (e.g. "installed") makes numbers look better than they mean.
Engagement
Ongoing, repeated use after activation. A user can activate once and never return — engagement asks if they keep getting value regularly.
Retention / Churn
Retention: % of a cohort still active over time, usually shown as a curve. Churn is its inverse — users who stop. The shape of the curve (flattening vs. ever-declining) matters more than any single point.
Cohort
A group of users defined by when they started (e.g. "merchants who activated in week 1"), tracked together over time so you're comparing like-for-like, not mixing new and old users.
North Star Metric
The single metric the whole team rallies around because it best reflects real customer value delivered — chosen carefully to avoid vanity metrics like raw usage counts.
Guardrail Metric
A metric that doesn't measure success directly but catches hidden harm — e.g. reopen rate catching quietly-bad AI replies that a rising accept-rate would otherwise mask.
WoW / MoM
Week-over-Week / Month-over-Month — the standard shorthand for comparing a metric to the prior equivalent period, used to spot trend direction, not just a snapshot.
Segmentation (on a dashboard)
Breaking an aggregate number down by a dimension (segment, ticket type, cohort, time) before explaining it — the core diagnostic habit that prevents jumping to the wrong conclusion.
Confound
When two segmentation cuts overlap so much that you can't tell which one is the real cause — e.g. if Segment 2 also happens to get more "damaged/lost" tickets, the segment-level and ticket-type-level findings may be the same root cause counted twice.
