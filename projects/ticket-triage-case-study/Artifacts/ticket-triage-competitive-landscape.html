Glossary
TAM / SAM / SOM
Three nested market-size estimates, from broadest (everyone with the problem) to narrowest (what you could realistically win in year one). Using TAM alone to justify a business case is a common, misleading mistake.
Direct vs. Indirect Competitor
Direct competitors solve the same problem the same way. Indirect competitors solve it a different way entirely (e.g. hiring help) — often the real alternative a customer is weighing, and easy to overlook.
Wedge
A specific, underserved angle in an already-contested market — not "no competition," but a real gap competitors leave open, usually due to their own business model or target customer constraints.
Positioning Matrix
A 2-axis visual plotting competitors against the two dimensions that matter most for the decision at hand — useful for spotting open quadrants, not for precise measurement.
